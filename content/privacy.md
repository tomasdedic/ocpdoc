## What is collected

Name and phone number or e-mail, when you call or write. Treatment notes, when you agree that they are kept.

## Why

To book your visit, to prepare for it, and to answer you. Nothing else. No advertising e-mails, no tracking scripts, no marketing cookies, no pixel from anybody.

## Who sees it

The therapist. Nobody else, and no scheduling service, because visits are booked by phone and e-mail. Payments are handled by the bank or the card terminal, never by this website.

## How long

Booking data: 3 years, because that is the limitation period for claims. Treatment notes: deleted on request.

## Your rights

Ask and you get a copy of what is stored, or a deletion. Contact: see the contact section of this page.

## Hosting

This site is static HTML. It is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which logs technical requests as described in their own privacy policy.
